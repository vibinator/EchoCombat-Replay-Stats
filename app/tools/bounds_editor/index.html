Play-area bounds

–

Click to place points around the play area. Click the first point (or press Enter) to close the outline.

Then: drag a point to move it, click a line to add a point there, right-click a point to remove it.

Wheel zooms, right-drag (or Space + drag) moves the view, F shows the whole map.

inside = in bounds   outside   where players are   current box

Your work is kept in this browser as you go; Save writes the file to hand over.
